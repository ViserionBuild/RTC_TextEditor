Real-Time Collaborative Text Editor 

Implement a sequence CRDT such as RGA, with unique character identifiers,
insertions, and deletion tombstones. Add a minimal editor and a relay that
persists and forwards operations between three clients. 
Allow offline editing and exchange missing operations after reconnection
using per-replica sequence summaries. Apply the same operations in 
shuffled, delayed, and duplicated orders and verify that document 
hashes converge. Demonstrate concurrent insertion and deletion at the 
same position, and report synchronization bytes and metadata growth; 
state when tombstones may safely be removed. Concepts: CRDTs, 
causality, strong eventual consistency, anti-entropy, idempotency, 
tombstones.
